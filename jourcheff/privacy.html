Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 04, 2026

🍳 JourCheff ("the app") is designed to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Data we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Any data shown in the app is processed on your device unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

Analytics & tracking

The app does not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your data.

Network

If a feature fetches live data (e.g. market prices), only the minimal request needed is sent over a secure connection. No personal identifiers are attached.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
